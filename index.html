Puzzle Circuit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy describes how Puzzle Circuit for Android and iOS handles information. References to “we,” “us,” and “our” mean the developer of Puzzle Circuit.

For questions or privacy requests, contact us at chase@sudokuhero.app.

2. Puzzle progress and preferences

Puzzle Circuit does not require an account. Saved puzzles, completed games, play times, statistics, streaks, and accessibility preferences are stored locally on your device.

The current app does not upload these records to a developer-operated server or provide an account-based cloud-sync service. Your device or operating system may include app data in backups according to your settings.

3. Advertising and consent

Puzzle Circuit integrates Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices.

The app currently requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data collection and device storage for purposes such as delivering ads, limiting repetition, measuring performance, and preventing fraud.

Depending on the platform, device permissions, consent choices, and ad-serving configuration, Google’s advertising services collect or process information such as:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Device and app identifiers.
  • App and advertisement interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Advertising consent choices and related signals.

The app does not request precise location permission for advertising. Consent-related choices and signals may be stored and communicated to Google.

Google uses advertising information to provide and measure advertising, analyze service performance, and prevent abuse. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services .

Advertisements and links may open third-party websites or services. Those services handle information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Your choices

Where required, the app presents advertising privacy choices before requesting ads. When available, use Settings → Advertising privacy → Manage ad privacy choices to review or change those choices.

You can also use your device’s advertising and privacy controls. Changing or declining consent may affect which ads can be shown; it does not prevent playing puzzles.

You can remove local game data by deleting the app and its data or using applicable device storage controls. Device backups may retain copies until you remove them separately. Removing the app does not automatically erase information already processed by advertising providers.

5. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot issues, and handle privacy requests.

Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Email is processed through the email services used to send and receive the correspondence.

6. Retention and security

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until removed or replaced. We retain support correspondence as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Google determines retention of information it processes under its policies. We do not have direct access to or control over all records held by Google.

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Audience

Puzzle Circuit is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through support correspondence. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact chase@sudokuhero.app so we can review and address the request.

8. Privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing.

Contact chase@sudokuhero.app to make a request about information held by us. We may need information to verify and respond to your request.

For information held by Google, use Google’s privacy controls or contact Google. You may also have a right to contact your local data protection authority.

9. This website

This privacy-policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added website analytics or advertising scripts.

GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses and technical request data, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
